## Screenshot requirement status

Authentic phone screenshots are **not generated in this repository package** to avoid fabricated UI claims.

Google Play phone screenshot requirement to satisfy manually:
- Portrait PNG/JPG
- **1080×1920** recommended capture target
- At least 2 screenshots

See `../testing/screenshot-capture-plan.md` for the exact scene list.
